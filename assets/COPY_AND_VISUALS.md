Eyebrow: **LONG-DURATION MISSION HEALTH INTELLIGENCE**

Hero: **Know the signal. Understand the trend. Explore the next step.**

Subhero: **ASTRA makes simulated astronaut health trends readable, explainable and actionable through four specialized monitoring agents.**

CTA: **Launch Mission**; secondary CTA: **See the science**.

Four agent captions:

- **01 Monitor** — Observe individual trends and data quality.
- **02 Assess** — Flag persistent deviations from personal baselines.
- **03 Explore** — Compare transparent, nonclinical 30-day planning assumptions.
- **04 Explain** — Trace every finding back to metrics, rules and research context.

Credibility note: **Independent NASA Space Apps 2026 participant prototype. Synthetic demonstration data; not NASA-endorsed or a medical device.**

## Visual asset specification

Produce the ASTRA logomark as **original simple orbital arc + pulse stroke** via SVG. No NASA typography/insignia/mission patches. Use CSS starfield, grid and fine orbital curves if desired; chart line colors should follow semantic palette in `docs/03_UI_UX.md`. Astronaut silhouettes optional, but avoid overly literal celebrity imagery or age-inappropriate visuals.

## No royalty/asset guessing

Use system fonts and open-source Lucide icons if installed; record library and license. Any external photo/video must have verified reuse license and attribution. Any AI-generated visual used in the final demo should carry required watermark and provenance.
